CalenTask

Smart Calendar & Focus Planner

About

CalenTask is a minimalist productivity app that lets you create task templates and schedule them directly in your Apple Calendar. Plan your day in seconds!

Features

  • One-tap task templates
  • Goals & routines
  • Pomodoro focus timer
  • Notes manager
  • Works offline
